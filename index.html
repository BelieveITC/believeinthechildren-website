Request Private Pay Services Our Story

Our mission is to enhance the lives of at-risk children by providing educational and family stabilization services to the adults in their lives, while taking every opportunity to discover, address, and focus on the needs of the child.

Who We Are

A Northern Illinois nonprofit dedicated to children, families, and caregivers. We provide professional, compassionate support that keeps the child's well-being at the center of everything we do.

Family Stabilization

Safe, neutral environments for supervised visits, parent time exchanges, and services that reduce conflict while protecting children.

Education & Support

Evidence-based parenting classes, fatherhood programs, and skill-building for caregivers and parents.

Community Focus

Serving Carroll, Lee, Ogle, Stephenson, and Whiteside Counties with services tailored to local families and DCFS partners.

Partner With Us

DCFS caseworkers: access our BelieveNet portal for referrals.
