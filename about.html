Our Story

Building support for children and families in Northern Illinois since 2019.

Founded to Keep Children at the Center

Believe In The Children was founded in 2019 by Patti Jennings in Forreston, Illinois. The organization grew from a clear recognition: children in crisis are best supported when the adults around them receive practical education, stabilization services, and a safe place to do hard work.

From the beginning, the mission has remained consistent — enhance the lives of at-risk children by providing educational and family stabilization services to the adults in their lives, while never losing sight of the child’s needs.

Growing to Meet Community Needs

The organization quickly identified a critical gap in Northern Illinois: few options existed for siblings to stay together during temporary foster placements. This led to the development of the 310 Sibling Home in Forreston, a residence designed so siblings could remain together with trained house parents during short-term needs.

Over the years, Believe In The Children has expanded services to include supervised visitation, parent time exchanges, evidence-based parenting education, the National Fatherhood Initiative’s 24/7 Dad program, counseling, transportation, and habilitation services. Partnerships with DCFS and community agencies have strengthened the ability to serve families across Carroll, Lee, Ogle, Stephenson, and Whiteside Counties.

Today

Believe In The Children continues to operate as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit from its Freeport location. Services are delivered with professionalism and compassion, whether through court-ordered or voluntary arrangements. The organization also partners with DCFS caseworkers through the BelieveNet portal to streamline referrals.

Leadership has evolved, with Derek Glover serving as Executive Director, building on the foundation established by founder Patti Jennings and a dedicated board and staff.

“It’s all about the children. Whether providing for the children themselves, their caregivers, or extended family, our focus never wavers from the child.”
